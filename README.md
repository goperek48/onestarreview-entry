A deadpan one-star review of an everyday essential — sleep — in one line.

- `main.py` — the entrypoint. Writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`.

The review (125 characters):

> One star. Sleep demands eight hours, total darkness, and silence, then wakes me at 3 a.m. to replay a conversation from 2014.

Why this one: sleep is the essential everyone relies on; the grievance is the
small daily annoyance everyone recognises (the 3 a.m. wake-up replaying an old
conversation); the complaint is read straight and the sting lands in the last
few words; one joke, told cleanly, no exclamation marks doing the work.
